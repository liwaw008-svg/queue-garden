# Interface identity

- Metaphor: civic ticket counter inside a greenhouse ledger.
- Entry: one tear-off sheet, opened by a single `PULL A TICKET` action.
- Composition: oversized editorial masthead, vertical paper queue, policy legend.
- Result: the next ticket becomes a physical sign; priorities tint the paper edge.
- Motion language: paper and queue progression, no cables, orbiting objects, wall bays, or card dashboard.
